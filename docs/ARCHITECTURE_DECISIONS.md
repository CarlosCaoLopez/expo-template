# Architecture Decisions

This document records the key decisions made during the development of **web-app**, including the
reasoning behind technology choices, the difficulties encountered, and the solutions adopted. It is
written honestly — including dead ends and trade-offs — so that future contributors can understand
the _why_ behind each decision, not just the _what_.

---

## How to add a new entry

Copy the template below and fill it in. Entries are listed in reverse chronological order (newest
first). Be honest: include alternatives you considered and why you discarded them.

```markdown
## [YYYY-MM-DD] Short title

**Context:** What was the situation or problem that triggered this decision?

**Decision:** What did we decide to do?

**Alternatives considered:** What else did we evaluate?

**Consequences:** What are the known trade-offs, risks, or follow-up work?
```
